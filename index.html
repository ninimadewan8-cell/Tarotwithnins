about me

Hello, I'm your reader ✨

I prioritise creating a safe, compassionate, and judgment‑free space where you can feel comfortable being open and honest about what you're experiencing.

My approach is to gently guide you through your life path — whether you're navigating struggles, facing obstacles, seeking clarity, or simply feeling unsure about your next step.

I want each reading to feel like a space where you can pause, breathe, express what's on your mind, and feel truly heard. Through the cards, we explore your situation together, bringing perspective and guidance while allowing you the space to arrive at your own understanding.

✨ Offered Readings ✨

🔮 Celtic Cross Reading

Every session I offer is a Celtic Cross reading — one of the most trusted and in‑depth spreads in tarot. Ten cards are laid out to explore your situation from every angle: where you are right now, what's challenging you, the roots of the matter, the recent past, what's possible ahead, the near future, your own attitude, outside influences, your hopes and fears, and the likely outcome.
